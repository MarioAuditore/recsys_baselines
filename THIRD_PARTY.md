# Third-party algorithms and attribution

## SASRec / Scalable Cross-Entropy

Reference: [AIRI-Institute/Scalable-SASRec](https://github.com/AIRI-Institute/Scalable-SASRec), commit `b1bd9e07e73bc612e16d4f5e8448cb5d1465bd01` (retrieved 2026-11-09).

The attention backbone and projection-bucket SCE in `src/recsys/models/sasrec.py` and `src/recsys/losses/sce.py` are adaptations of `model.py`. The original MIT license is retained in `third_party/Scalable-SASRec-LICENSE`.

Changes: valid positions are removed before SCE selection; only effective catalog embeddings participate; zero-valued losses are retained when a position was selected; bucket sizes are bounded by the available positions/classes; padding is outside the catalog; right padding and explicit key masks prevent empty attention rows; data preparation/evaluation are independent implementations. These are not claims of reproducing the paper's exact experimental results.

Please cite Gleb Mezentsev, Danil Gusak, Ivan Oseledets, Evgeny Frolov, “Scalable Cross-Entropy Loss for Sequential Recommendations with Large Item Catalogs”, RecSys 2024, and Wang-Cheng Kang, Julian McAuley, “Self-Attentive Sequential Recommendation”, ICDM 2018.

## UltraGCN

Reference: [RecZoo / UltraGCN](https://github.com/reczoo/RecZoo/tree/88fae23ff0b8321c60655d9c21e0a1bb1173f6af/matching/gnn/UltraGCN), commit `88fae23ff0b8321c60655d9c21e0a1bb1173f6af`.

Copyright (C) 2020-2023. The UltraGCN Authors. All rights reserved.
Licensed under Apache License 2.0; see `third_party/Apache-2.0.txt`.

The objective and constraint weighting in `src/recsys/models/graph.py` and `neighbors.py` follow the reference implementation. Changes: compute item-item neighbors in sparse blocks instead of a dense quadratic constraint matrix; guard zero degrees; use stable softplus; deterministic neighbor ties; default negative sampling excludes observed pairs; no upstream utility/evaluation code is copied. The positive terms and item constraint are summed, negative samples averaged, matching the reference reduction.

Please cite Kelong Mao, Jieming Zhu, Xi Xiao, Biao Lu, Zhaowei Wang, Xiuqiang He, “UltraGCN: Ultra Simplification of Graph Convolutional Networks for Recommendation”, CIKM 2021.

## Other algorithms and data

LightGCN implements the normalized propagation and BPR objective from Xiangnan He et al., “LightGCN: Simplifying and Powering Graph Convolution Network for Recommendation”, SIGIR 2020. PureSVD follows the truncated-SVD top-N approach of Paolo Cremonesi, Yehuda Koren, Roberto Turrin, RecSys 2010. These are independent implementations.

Real datasets are downloaded separately, not redistributed. Their original terms apply. Source URLs/revisions/checksums are recorded in download and preparation manifests. CSV examples generated by tests are synthetic.
